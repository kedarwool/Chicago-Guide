Events

Chicago always has something going on, especially during the warmer months. From music festivals to food events and lakefront shows, these events give visitors a good reason to spend more time exploring the city.

Date Event Location Description
June Chicago Blues Festival Millennium Park A free outdoor music festival that celebrates Chicago’s blues history with live performances in the downtown area.
July Taste of Chicago Grant Park A popular food festival where visitors can try dishes from local restaurants, listen to music, and enjoy the downtown atmosphere.
August Chicago Air and Water Show North Avenue Beach One of the city’s biggest summer events, featuring aircraft performances, lakefront crowds, and great views along the beach.

Before You Go

  • Check the official event website for exact dates, times, and weather updates.
  • Use public transportation when possible because parking can be difficult downtown.
  • Arrive early for free events since popular areas can fill up fast.
  • Bring water, comfortable shoes, and a phone charger if you plan to stay out for a while.
